LoveDoodle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

The short version

LoveDoodle has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your doodles travel between your two iPhones through Apple's iCloud. We never see them on our own servers because we don't run any.

What the app stores

  • Doodles and notes you send to your partner, with the time they were sent and whether they are hidden under scratch foil.
  • Mood and "social battery" values you choose to share with your partner.
  • A random device ID generated by the app, used to pair your phone with your partner's. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or email.
  • Couple Pass status (whether a subscription is active and when it ends), so your partner's phone can unlock premium too.

This data is stored in Apple's CloudKit (iCloud) under the LoveDoodle app container and on your device. It is only used to make the app work.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives the random device ID and your App Store purchase receipt, never your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts or photo library access.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app alerts you when your partner sends a doodle and can send a daily streak reminder. You can turn these off in iOS Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Tap Unpair from Partner in Settings to stop sharing, and delete the app to remove local data. To delete the doodles stored in iCloud, email us and we will remove them within 30 days.

Children

LoveDoodle is not directed at children under 13.
