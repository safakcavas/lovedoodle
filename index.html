LoveDoodle

Draw on your partner's lock screen. Pair two iPhones with a 6-digit code and send doodles that appear in each other's widgets.

Getting started

Open LoveDoodle on both iPhones. One of you shares the pairing code, the other enters it. Then long-press your lock screen, tap Customize, and add the LoveDoodle widget under the clock.

Doodles aren't arriving?

Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud and notifications are allowed for LoveDoodle. Widgets usually update within a few seconds, but iOS can delay them in Low Power Mode. Opening the app refreshes it right away.

Couple Pass

One subscription unlocks unlimited doodles for both of you. Manage or cancel it anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, tap Restore on the Couple Pass screen.
